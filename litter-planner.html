Earnings are worked out from each litter's puppy count × its own sale price. Income tax and the Medicare levy are estimates only, using current ATO resident rates applied to the whole year's net profit — they don't account for any other income you have, tax offsets like LITO, or the Medicare levy low-income reduction. Check with your accountant before relying on these figures.
